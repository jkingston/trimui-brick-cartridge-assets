# Asset licensing

The MIT licence covers the source code in `tools/`, `tests/` and `preview/`.
It does not replace the per-source terms recorded for reference photographs,
label artwork, CAD references or other embedded material.

`catalogue/artwork.json` records the source URL, checksum, author and licence
when supplied for each curated image. Release indices preserve that provenance
and describe the transformations applied. Game artwork, product designs and
trademarks remain the property of their respective owners; no blanket licence
is asserted over them.

Editable model provenance and known accuracy limits are recorded in
`sources/catalogue.json` and `catalogue/models.json`.
